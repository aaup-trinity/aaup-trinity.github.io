Who we are

We are a union of scholars and educators who teach and do research at Trinity University in San Antonio, Texas. As chapter members, we are also members of the national AAUP and the Texas AAUP-AFT. We advocate and organize to build labor power in higher education, advance academic freedom and shared governance, promote the economic security of students and academic professionals, and cultivate a higher education community that contributes to the common good.

Join

Become a member today by joining the national AAUP. Note that if you teach in Texas, you join Texas AAUP through the combined Texas AAUP-AFT membership form.

Membership in our chapter is open to all current and retired faculty, full- or part-time researchers, graduate students, or academic professionals at Trinity University. We encourage all eligible members of the Trinity Community to join AAUP and our local chapter. By joining AAUP, you support organizing and advocacy for academic freedom, shared governance, and labor rights in two critical ways:

  1. Your dues support national and chapter-level activities.
  2. Your membership is a statement of solidarity and a commitment toward our union's efforts. As a dues-paying member, you help demonstrate our collective organizing power.

We're proud to be members of our union! Please contact any of the Executive Committee or other members to learn first-hand why AAUP membership is important for academics and provides real-world benefits to your career.

What we're doing

Advocating

Organizing

…to build collective power within Trinity and through coalitions with other San Antonio AAUP chapters.

Writing
